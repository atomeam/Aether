## Overview

Moon Child is a nostalgic platform game that brings classic 16-bit era gaming to the Pocket PC. With 13 levels across 4 worlds, unique controls, and crisp graphics, it's a love letter to the golden age of platformers.

---

## Story

> *"In another time, in another place, a green elf is sent by the moon to protect the planet Utopia. They call him Moon Child. Danger threatens. One night..."*

A classic hero's journey setup that immediately establishes the stakes. The green elf Moon Child must protect Utopia from impending danger.

---

## Gameplay

### The Hook
**Objective:** Collect 77 dark diamonds and find the way out of each level.

### Structure
- **13 levels** across **4 worlds** (4 levels per world + final level)
- **Jigsaw puzzle level selection** - Unique visual interface
- **Non-linear progression** - Start with any of the 12 main levels
- **Level flipping mechanics** - Unlock doors and solve puzzles

### Controls
**Two control schemes:**
1. **Traditional:** D-Pad for movement (landscape orientation required)
2. **Innovative:** Tap screen with right thumb or stylus to move and jump

The stylus/tap control is a brilliant adaptation for Pocket PC, offering precision that D-Pads can't match.

### Difficulty
- **Progressive difficulty** - Each level gets harder
- **Optical illusions** - The game messes with your perception (platforms that seem to go nowhere, level transitions that confuse)
- **No weapons** - Pure platforming, no combat
- **Enemies:** Snakes and other creatures (some hard to see on small screens)

## Verdict

**Score: 8.5/10**

Moon Child is a standout platformer for Pocket PC that successfully captures the essence of classic 16-bit gaming while innovating with Pocket PC-specific features. The dual control scheme (D-Pad + stylus) is particularly smart, making the game accessible to different play styles.

The optical illusion level design is both a strength and weakness - it's clever but can be frustrating. The graphics are genuinely impressive for the platform, and the performance tuning options ensure it runs on a wide range of devices.

**Recommendation:** If you enjoy platformers and have a Pocket PC, Moon Child is a must-have. It's one of the best examples of the genre on the platform and offers hours of nostalgic fun. Just make sure you have 11 MB of storage and play in a well-lit area to see all the details.

**Quote:** "Mario has nothing on Moon Child!" - High praise indeed.

## Quick Tips

1. **Set volume before playing** - No in-game control
2. **Try the stylus controls** - More precise than D-Pad
3. **Play in good lighting** - Some enemies are hard to see
4. **Start with easier levels** - Get used to controls before tackling hard ones
5. **Use performance options** - Tune for your specific device
6. **Watch for optical illusions** - Things aren't always what they seem
