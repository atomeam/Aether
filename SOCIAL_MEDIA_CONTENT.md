### Post 2: Case Study
**Headline:** How I Cut My Infrastructure Costs by 40% (And You Can Too)

Last month, I looked at my cloud bill and realized I was spending $400/month on infrastructure I wasn't fully using. As a solo developer, every dollar counts.

Here's what I did:

**Step 1: Get Real Usage Data**
I set up monitoring to track CPU, memory, database performance, and storage.

**What I found:** CPU never exceeded 30%, memory never went above 40%, storage was only 20% full.

**Step 2: Right-Size the Database**
Downgraded from db.t3.large to db.t3.medium.
**Savings:** $70/month

**Step 3: Consolidate App Servers**
Consolidated to one server with auto-scaling.
**Savings:** $60/month

**Step 4: Optimize Storage and Bandwidth**
Switched to standard storage and tiered bandwidth.
**Savings:** $40/month

**The Result:**
- Before: $400/month
- After: $240/month
- Savings: $160/month (40% reduction)
- Performance: No degradation
