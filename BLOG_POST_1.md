# 5 Signs Your Solo Developer Infrastructure Needs Automation

As a solo developer, you're wearing all the hats - developer, DevOps, security analyst, and support team. But when it comes to infrastructure monitoring, are you spending more time fixing servers than building features?

Here are 5 signs your infrastructure needs automation:

## 1. You're Missing Production Issues Until Users Report Them

The classic "it works on my machine" problem. You deploy your app, go to sleep, and wake up to angry emails from users who couldn't access your service.

**The problem:** Manual monitoring means you only know about issues when users tell you.

**The solution:** Automated monitoring catches issues before your users do, giving you time to fix them proactively.

## 2. You're Waking Up at 3 AM to Fix Server Crashes

We've all been there - a server crashes, your phone buzzes with alerts, and you're debugging production issues in the middle of the night.

**The problem:** Reactive monitoring means you're always putting out fires instead of preventing them.

**The solution:** Predictive monitoring and auto-remediation can fix common issues before they become critical.

## 3. You Don't Know Which Features Are Causing Performance Issues

Your app is slow, but you don't know if it's the database, the API, or the frontend. You spend hours guessing and testing.

**The problem:** Without detailed metrics, you're optimizing blindly.

**The solution:** Granular monitoring shows you exactly which components are slow, so you can optimize what matters.

## 4. You're Over-Provisioning Resources "Just to Be Safe"

You're paying for 2x the server capacity you actually need because you're afraid of traffic spikes.

**The problem:** Without usage data, you're wasting money on unused resources.

**The solution:** Real-time usage tracking helps you right-size your infrastructure and cut costs.

## 5. You Have No Idea When You'll Hit Your Limits

You're growing, but you don't know when you'll hit database connection limits, API rate limits, or storage capacity.

**The problem:** Growth planning is guesswork without data.

**The solution:** Trend analysis and forecasting helps you plan infrastructure scaling before you hit limits.
